# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please do not open a public issue for security vulnerabilities.**

Instead, email **security@kanninja.app** with:

- A description of the issue and its impact
- Steps to reproduce (proof of concept if possible)
- Your name and contact info (for follow-up and credit, if you want it)

You'll get an acknowledgement within **72 hours**. We aim to triage within 7 days and ship a fix for confirmed issues within 30 days, depending on severity.

## Scope

In scope:

- The hosted product at `kanninja.app`
- The code in this repository (frontend, backend, MCP server, shared)

Out of scope:

- Third-party services we depend on (Clerk, Supabase, Stripe, OpenAI) — report those to the respective vendors
- Denial-of-service attacks, social engineering, or physical attacks
- Issues requiring physical access to a user's device

## Safe harbor

We will not pursue legal action against researchers who:

- Report in good faith through the channel above
- Do not access, modify, or exfiltrate data belonging to others
- Do not degrade service for real users
- Give us reasonable time to fix the issue before public disclosure

Thank you for helping keep kanNINJA and its users safe.
