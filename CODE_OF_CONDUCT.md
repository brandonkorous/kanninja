# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a welcoming place to contribute, regardless of experience level, background, or identity. In the interest of making that real, we pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Standards

Examples of behavior we want:

- Respectful, constructive feedback
- Patience with newcomers
- Assuming good intent
- Disagreeing about ideas, not people

Examples of behavior we won't tolerate:

- Personal attacks, insults, or derogatory comments
- Harassment, public or private
- Publishing others' private information
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to **conduct@kanninja.app**. All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Project maintainers have the right to remove, edit, or reject contributions, comments, commits, and issues that violate this code, and to ban contributors temporarily or permanently for behavior they deem inappropriate.
